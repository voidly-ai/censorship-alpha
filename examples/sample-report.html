Methodology

This report combines two data sources:

  • Voidly (voidly.ai) — Real-time internet censorship monitoring across 15 countries. Tests 8 major crypto exchanges for DNS, TCP, TLS, and HTTP blocking using OONI, CensoredPlanet, and community probe data.
  • Nansen (nansen.ai) — On-chain analytics tracking smart money flows, DEX activity, and exchange wallet balances across Ethereum, BNB Chain, Solana, and Base.
  • Ground-truth sources — Exchange blocking data enriched with verified reports from Chainalysis 2026 Crypto Crime Report, CoinGecko, Cloudwards, and DeFi Race.

The Censorship Impact Score combines exchange block count (35%), country risk tier (25%), censorship severity (25%), and 7-day forecast risk (15%). Exchange blocking data merges real-time API monitoring with verified ground-truth from published regulatory and research sources.
